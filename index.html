Research

Three threads, one question: how do we know an animal is well? I work where machine learning meets the barn floor, from model design to farmer interviews.

01 · Vision

AI for animal welfare

Computer vision for behaviour recognition and automated welfare assessment, with attention to whether systems are ready to leave the lab.

  • Computer vision
  • Behaviour recognition

Precision livestock farming

Wearable sensors, edge AI and signal processing for continuous, low-cost monitoring of individual animals.

  • Wearables
  • TinyML / Edge AI
  • Time-series
